# Place Your Alcohol Stimulus Images Here

Add alcohol-related images to this folder:
- Beer bottles, cans, or glasses
- Wine bottles or glasses  
- Liquor bottles
- Cocktails
- Mixed drinks

**Supported formats:** .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .bmp, .gif, .tiff, .webp

Example filenames:
- beer_bottle_1.jpg
- wine_glass_red.jpg
- whiskey_bottle.jpg
- cocktail_martini.jpg

The images will automatically be loaded when you run the experiment.
