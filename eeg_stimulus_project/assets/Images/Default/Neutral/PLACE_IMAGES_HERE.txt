# Place Your Neutral Stimulus Images Here

Add neutral (non-alcoholic) images to this folder:
- Water bottles or glasses
- Juice bottles or cartons
- Soft drink cans or bottles
- Coffee or tea cups
- Other beverage containers

**Supported formats:** .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .bmp, .gif, .tiff, .webp

Example filenames:
- water_bottle_1.jpg
- orange_juice_glass.jpg
- soda_can_1.jpg
- coffee_cup.jpg

The images will automatically be loaded when you run the experiment.
